**Astro** is a web framework designed for **content-driven websites** such as blogs, marketing websites, and e-commerce sites. Astro is known for introducing a new [frontend architecture](https://docs.astro.build/en/concepts/islands/) that reduces JavaScript overhead and complexity compared with other frameworks. If you need a website that loads fast and has good SEO, Astro is for you.
